REGULATORY — SOURCE-BOUND PROJECT METHODOLOGY

Scope: general editorial method, not verified directory requirements, ranking criteria or submission evidence. Apply only to the relevant part of a mixed practice. The target template and verified guide control fields and categories.

Identify authority, regulated activity, represented party, instrument and procedural stage from the source. Advice, permits, procurement challenges and judicial review are different mandates. Preserve whether a measure is proposed, in force, contested or final. Describe the actual legal consequence without inventing policy influence or industry-wide precedent. Do not import an authority or procedure from a different jurisdiction.

Planning and writing responsibilities
Planning compares supported mandates without quotas and records shared selection and candidacy decisions. A writing unit consumes those decisions; it must not reselect matters or change categories. Build the Submission and internal Audit from the same facts with different voices. Preserve source-supported achievements, limitations and current status together. Missing evidence is an explicit gap, not an invented result. Never import names, examples or figures from methodology into the client's documents.
