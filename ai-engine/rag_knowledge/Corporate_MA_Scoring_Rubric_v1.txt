# Corporate/M&A Scoring Rubric — RankPilot RAG v1

## Purpose

This rubric gives RankPilot a structured way to score Corporate/M&A matters.

The score is not a ranking prediction. It is an internal diagnostic tool to determine whether a matter is strong evidence for a directory submission.

Maximum score: 100 points.

## 1. Correct category fit — 10 points

10 = clearly belongs in Corporate/M&A
7 = mostly Corporate/M&A with some adjacent practice overlap
4 = Corporate-adjacent but may belong elsewhere
0 = wrong practice area

Red flags:

- financing work presented as M&A
- tax-led reorganisation presented as M&A
- antitrust work presented as M&A
- project development presented as M&A
- routine corporate housekeeping presented as M&A

## 2. Strategic importance — 15 points

15 = transformational, strategic or market-shaping transaction
11 = clearly important to client or market
7 = commercially relevant but not exceptional
3 = routine business transaction
0 = no strategic significance visible

Indicators:

- market entry
- acquisition of strategic asset
- consolidation in sector
- major client expansion
- sale of core business
- restructuring of ownership or control

## 3. Client prestige / market visibility — 15 points

15 = market-leading, elite, listed, institutional, multinational or highly visible client
11 = recognised client or strong market participant
7 = credible mid-market client
3 = limited client profile
0 = no client or client profile

If confidential, RankPilot should require a profile description.

## 4. Firm role strength — 15 points

15 = lead counsel / strategic architect / negotiation lead
11 = major counsel with substantive responsibility
7 = important but limited role
3 = local or support counsel
0 = unclear role

Strong roles:

- buyer counsel
- seller counsel
- sponsor counsel
- lead transaction counsel
- counsel coordinating multiple jurisdictions
- counsel leading negotiation

Weak unless explained:

- due diligence support
- local counsel
- closing support
- corporate housekeeping

## 5. Transaction complexity — 15 points

15 = highly complex transaction
11 = strong complexity
7 = some complexity
3 = limited complexity
0 = no visible complexity

Complexity indicators:

- cross-border structure
- regulated sector
- auction process
- antitrust/foreign investment approval
- carve-out
- earn-out
- complex shareholder agreements
- management rollover
- distressed asset
- multiple stakeholders
- public company rules
- financing dependency
- governance redesign

## 6. Economic value / relative market significance — 10 points

10 = high-value or highly significant for the market
7 = good value / strong mid-market
4 = modest value
0 = no value or value band

Important:

Deal value is not enough. A lower-value matter can score highly if strategically complex.

## 7. Lawyer leadership visibility — 10 points

10 = named lead partners and senior team with clear roles
7 = partners named but roles incomplete
4 = team mentioned generically
0 = no lawyer visibility

RankPilot must push for named lawyers where possible.

## 8. Market / competitive impact — 10 points

10 = matter changes market positioning or competitive perception
7 = supports strong practice narrative
4 = useful but ordinary
0 = no market impact

Indicators:

- confirms elite status
- shows move into higher-value work
- supports new sector positioning
- demonstrates impact of lateral hire
- evidences growth trajectory
- differentiates from competitors

## Final classification

### 85-100: Flagship Corporate/M&A matter

Use at the top of submission. Strong for editorial and lawyer positioning.

### 70-84: Strong Corporate/M&A matter

Include and polish narrative.

### 55-69: Useful but underdeveloped matter

May be strong if missing facts are added.

### 40-54: Weak matter

Use only for volume, client continuity or strategic coverage.

### 0-39: Low-value or wrong-fit matter

Omit, move to another practice or rewrite only if additional facts materially improve it.

## Directory weighting adjustments

### Chambers

Increase weight for:

- client prestige
- lawyer leadership
- market impact
- strategic role
- reputation signal

### Legal 500

Increase weight for:

- correct category fit
- breadth
- consistency
- team depth
- commerciality
- value band accuracy

### IFLR1000

Increase weight for:

- transaction architecture
- financial/corporate engineering
- private equity
- LBOs
- spin-offs
- public bids
- complex structures

### Leaders League

Increase weight for:

- reputation
- visibility
- client prestige
- differentiation
- team growth
- peer recognition
