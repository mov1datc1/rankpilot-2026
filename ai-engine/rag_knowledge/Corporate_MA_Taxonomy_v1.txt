# Corporate/M&A Taxonomy — RankPilot RAG v1

## Purpose

This document gives RankPilot a classification system for Corporate/M&A matters.

RankPilot must classify matters precisely before scoring or rewriting them.

## Core transaction types

### Acquisition

Purchase of a company, asset, business unit, shareholding or portfolio.

Signals:

- buyer or seller representation
- share purchase agreement
- asset purchase agreement
- closing mechanics
- due diligence
- indemnities
- regulatory approvals

High-ranking indicators:

- strategic acquisition
- high-value target
- regulated sector
- cross-border component
- auction process
- complex financing
- difficult closing conditions
- sophisticated indemnity structure

### Disposal

Sale of a company, asset, subsidiary, business line or shareholding.

High-ranking indicators:

- strategic divestment
- competitive sale process
- complex carve-out
- regulatory approvals
- transitional services
- retained liabilities
- governance or shareholder issues

### Merger

Combination of two or more entities.

High-ranking indicators:

- transformational market effect
- regulatory approval complexity
- integration challenges
- shareholder approvals
- governance restructuring
- public company component

### Joint venture

Creation or restructuring of a jointly owned commercial vehicle or business arrangement.

High-ranking indicators:

- complex governance
- deadlock mechanisms
- exit rights
- contribution structures
- regulated industry
- cross-border partner alignment
- long-term strategic alliance

### Private equity transaction

Transaction involving a private equity sponsor.

Includes:

- buyout
- secondary buyout
- exit
- portfolio company acquisition
- minority investment
- growth investment
- management rollover
- co-investment

High-ranking indicators:

- sponsor prestige
- competitive auction
- leveraged acquisition
- management incentive plan
- sophisticated financing
- complex shareholder arrangements

### Corporate restructuring / reorganisation

Non-distressed corporate restructuring or reorganisation.

Includes:

- group reorganisation
- spin-off
- carve-out
- post-acquisition integration
- pre-sale restructuring
- cross-border simplification

High-ranking indicators:

- multinational scope
- tax/governance complexity
- regulatory impact
- multiple entities
- business transformation

### Distressed M&A

Acquisition or sale of distressed assets or companies.

High-ranking indicators:

- insolvency risk
- creditor involvement
- compressed timeline
- regulatory pressure
- asset preservation
- execution risk
- rescue transaction

### Minority investment

Investment involving less than control.

High-ranking indicators:

- investor protections
- governance rights
- veto rights
- exit rights
- valuation complexity
- founder/investor alignment
- preferred shares or special rights

### Strategic commercial expansion

Corporate transaction supporting entry into a new market, sector or business line.

High-ranking indicators:

- market entry
- regulatory barriers
- institutional counterparties
- strategic partnership
- cross-border expansion
- new product or industry vertical

### Entity formation

Creation of companies or vehicles.

Usually low-ranking unless linked to:

- complex governance
- regulated investment structure
- joint venture
- holding company structure
- cross-border expansion
- investment platform

### Governance matter

Corporate governance work connected to ownership, control or strategic decision-making.

High-ranking indicators:

- shareholder conflict prevention
- control rights
- board structure
- minority protections
- founder disputes
- governance redesign after investment

## Client-side taxonomy

RankPilot must classify whether the firm acted for:

- buyer
- seller
- target
- founder
- controlling shareholder
- minority shareholder
- private equity sponsor
- portfolio company
- strategic investor
- financial adviser
- management team
- joint venture partner
- family office
- sovereign or state-owned entity

## Role taxonomy

### Very strong roles

- lead counsel
- buyer counsel
- seller counsel
- strategic transaction counsel
- lead negotiation counsel
- transaction architect
- counsel coordinating multiple jurisdictions
- counsel to sponsor on full acquisition

### Moderate roles

- local counsel with substantive role
- counsel on corporate governance aspects
- counsel on one transaction component
- counsel to minority investor
- counsel on closing or regulatory mechanics

### Weak roles unless explained

- due diligence support
- local counsel only
- ancillary corporate advice
- document review
- corporate housekeeping
- entity formation only

## Complexity taxonomy

RankPilot should detect:

- cross-border structure
- multiple parties
- regulated sector
- antitrust approval
- foreign investment approval
- public company rules
- shareholder approvals
- auction process
- earn-out mechanism
- deferred consideration
- escrow and indemnity complexity
- management rollover
- carve-out
- transitional services agreement
- complex governance rights
- distressed or accelerated timeline
- political or institutional sensitivity
- integration complexity
- financing dependency
- tax-sensitive structure

## Adjacent practice area risks

RankPilot must check whether the matter belongs elsewhere or requires different framing.

### Banking & Finance

If the core issue is:

- financing structure
- debt architecture
- syndication
- collateral
- refinancing
- lenders/borrowers

### Competition

If the core issue is:

- merger clearance
- antitrust approval
- concentration analysis
- regulatory review

### Tax

If the core issue is:

- tax structuring
- transfer pricing
- tax-led reorganisation
- tax dispute

### Projects / Infrastructure / Energy

If the core issue is:

- concession
- infrastructure asset
- public procurement
- project development
- sector regulation

### Disputes

If the core issue is:

- shareholder dispute
- post-M&A conflict
- indemnity claim
- arbitration
- enforcement
