LABOUR & EMPLOYMENT — PROJECT REASONING REFERENCE

Scope
This document supplies editorial reasoning, not verified Chambers rules or jurisdiction-specific thresholds. Match the current submission's directory, practice, jurisdiction and actual represented side. Do not import another country's categories, monetary limits, ranking segments or assumptions. Only supplied verified criteria can establish those requirements. This reference contains no model client facts or preferred hero matter.

Understand the legal work
Distinguish individual employment advice and disputes from collective labour relations without assuming they must be separate ranking categories. Relevant work can include workforce integration, employment litigation, collective bargaining, strike prevention, regulatory response, compliance, executive advice and industrial relations. Evaluate the actual legal difficulty, intervention and outcome in their context; do not impose a checklist of capabilities absent from the source.

Represented side and institutional thesis
Identify whether the mandate represents an employer, employee, executive or union. Evaluate its fit with the confirmed practice thesis and the selection policy. Do not disguise employee-side work as employer-side advice through a change of wording. A high claim amount alone does not establish strategic fit. Conversely, documented volume work can demonstrate substantive coordination and legal contribution; do not dismiss it merely as routine.

Develop distinctive evidence
Connect the operational objective to the specific legal obstacle, the firm's action and the documented result or current stage. Distinguish managing proceedings from winning them, preventing a strike from ending one, intended risk reduction from an achieved result, and acquisition context from work actually performed on labour integration. Preserve the source's decisive workforce scale, locations, proceedings and economic concepts in the permitted confidential matter narrative. Public summaries preserve the contribution without exposing restricted identifying combinations. A project value is not automatically a labour claim, liability, fee or saving.

Personal attribution and portfolio comparison
Show the actual lawyers' supported roles while keeping firm acts and personal acts distinct. Compare matters by incremental legal capability, outcomes, execution and personal evidence, not a predetermined client hierarchy. Select complementary strengths when the sources justify them; do not force every case into a fixed number of institutional pillars. The Audit and Submission must execute the same selected evidence and personal argument. Use natural narrative with no fixed paragraph template, fabricated precedent, ornamental praise or invented market comparison.
