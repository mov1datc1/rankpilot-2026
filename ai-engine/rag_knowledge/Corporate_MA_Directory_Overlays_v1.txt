# Corporate/M&A Directory Overlays — RankPilot RAG v1

## Purpose

This document defines how Corporate/M&A analysis should change depending on the target directory.

The same matter may have different ranking value for Chambers, Legal 500, IFLR1000 and Leaders League.

## Chambers Corporate/M&A overlay

### Editorial personality

Chambers is reputation-heavy, prestige-sensitive and sophistication-driven.

It cares strongly about:

- market standing
- client feedback
- peer recognition
- prestige of clients
- visible strategic matters
- lawyer leadership
- complexity
- market impact
- partner-level reputation

### RankPilot should prioritise

- Was the firm in a lead strategic role?
- Was the client prestigious or market-visible?
- Does the matter reinforce market perception?
- Are named partners visibly leading the work?
- Is there a strong referee/client feedback opportunity?
- Does the matter show sophistication beyond routine execution?

### Chambers weakness signals

- matter is only a list item
- no named partner
- no clear firm role
- no strategic importance
- no client prestige or profile
- no explanation of market impact
- excessive reliance on deal value

## Legal 500 Corporate/M&A overlay

### Editorial personality

Legal 500 is taxonomy-sensitive, practice-coverage driven and category-discipline oriented.

It cares strongly about:

- correct category segmentation
- breadth of corporate capability
- consistency of mandates
- quality of work
- sector diversity
- client service
- team depth
- matter qualification

### RankPilot should prioritise

- Does the matter fit the exact Legal 500 category?
- Is it high-end, mid-market, smaller deal, private equity or venture capital?
- Is the work Corporate/M&A rather than finance, tax, competition or projects?
- Does the submission show consistent activity and breadth?
- Does the team demonstrate commerciality and responsiveness?

### Legal 500 weakness signals

- wrong value band
- wrong category
- work belongs in private equity, venture capital or finance
- overemphasis on supporting roles
- insufficient team depth
- limited breadth of service
- no clear commercial value to client

## IFLR1000 Corporate/M&A overlay

### Editorial personality

IFLR1000 is transactional, technical and financial-corporate in orientation.

It cares strongly about:

- public bids
- acquisitions
- LBOs
- MBOs
- reverse buyouts
- partial takeovers
- share transfers
- spin-offs
- joint ventures
- private equity M&A
- portfolio management
- complex transactional engineering

### RankPilot should prioritise

- What is the transaction structure?
- What was technically complex?
- Was it public M&A, private equity, LBO, spin-off or sophisticated JV?
- Was there acquisition financing or complex corporate architecture?
- Did the firm handle transaction engineering?
- Was the matter financially significant?

### IFLR weakness signals

- generic commercial expansion
- routine entity formation
- low technical complexity
- no financial structure
- no transactional architecture
- limited corporate engineering

## Leaders League Corporate/M&A overlay

### Editorial personality

Leaders League is positioning, reputation and ecosystem-driven.

It relies on:

- submissions
- interviews
- client feedback
- peer feedback
- qualified third-party market review

It cares strongly about:

- recognition in the market
- client prestige
- differentiation
- matter quality
- volume and size
- innovation
- team size and expansion
- partner reputation
- internationalisation
- business ecosystem visibility

### RankPilot should prioritise

- Does the firm feel differentiated?
- Does the client base show prestige?
- Does the team appear visible in the market?
- Is there evidence of momentum?
- Are lateral hires or team growth positioned strategically?
- Does the submission explain why the firm stands out from competitors?

### Leaders League weakness signals

- no differentiation from competitors
- no visibility narrative
- no third-party validation
- weak client portfolio
- no team growth or momentum
- matter list without market positioning
