LAWYER LEADERSHIP — SOURCE-BOUND PROJECT METHODOLOGY

Evaluate both the institutional case and the individual case. For each supplied person, distinguish professional seniority, target-practice specialization, personally attributed mandates, demonstrated contribution, career credentials, independent recognition when supplied, and the proposed category. These are separate dimensions; no one substitutes for all the others. A title or prestigious client alone does not prove a personal achievement or ranking eligibility.

Separate leadership from execution
Maintain three distinct propositions: who led the mandate, who performed a specific action, and who achieved the reported outcome. A lead-lawyer field supports responsibility for the mandate; it does not personally attribute every action performed by the firm. When the original says the firm designed a strategy and names a lead lawyer separately, preserve both propositions without converting them into a sentence saying the person designed it. Conversely, retain a specific personal act when the source actually attributes it. A team listing supports participation, not individual leadership or a personal outcome. A template heading does not establish partner seniority.

Build a cumulative personal argument
Connect material personally attributed work to the candidate's supported expertise and contribution. Use legal difficulty, action and actual result rather than repeating a service list. Pertinent reserve mandates may support a person even when they are not part of the final core portfolio; excluded or unrelated work does not automatically qualify. Preserve substantiated achievements, current pending work and material credentials together. Keep nomination decisions separate from the quality of a factual biography. Follow the task's depth requirements without inventing personal contributions or padding a limited profile.

Human leadership dimensions
Consider relationship continuity, negotiation responsibility, technical contribution, coordination, independent visibility and team depth when supported. Identify who actually did what. Repeated client mandates can establish recurrence, not automatic relationship ownership or market reputation. Do not infer dependence on one person, weak succession or the absence of institutional capability merely because the submission does not describe others. Distinguish a gap in submitted evidence from a fact about the firm.

Categories and research
Evaluate categories against the target directory, practice, verified criteria and supported seniority. Do not mix category names from different directories or infer age-based eligibility. Distinguish current declared rank, verified rank, a proposed candidacy and a prediction. If a criterion is unavailable, record the precise unresolved criterion internally rather than inventing a category or downgrading a valid biography. Career credentials and external recognition complement, but do not replace, mandate evidence. Supplied client feedback may support a recommendation; reference-methodology text is not client feedback.

Consistency between outputs
The Audit explains the category recommendation, personal evidence, comparative strength and genuine remaining action. B9 develops the supported personal contribution in the firm's voice, with public confidentiality safeguards. The same legal contribution and achieved result must not disappear between those outputs. Comparative priorities should reflect the actual evidence; do not impose a fixed number of principal candidates or spread matters equally as a quota.
