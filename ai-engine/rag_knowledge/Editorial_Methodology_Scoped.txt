EDITORIAL METHODOLOGY — SCOPED PROJECT REFERENCE

Authority and evidence
This reference explains a method; it cannot override the task, field contract, source restrictions or confirmed user decisions. It is not an official directory rule, external market research, client evidence or approval of a submission. Original matter records and professional profiles supply facts. Reference examples and historical submissions supply no facts about the current client. A requested target is an objective, not current recognition or a predicted result.

One evidence case, different document purposes
Submission B9 describes each person's supported credentials, actual role and distinctive legal contribution in the firm's voice. B10 develops the department's evidenced capabilities and achievements. C2 makes a grounded argument for the requested coverage, distinct from B10. Matter narratives connect client objective, legal obstacle, firm action, supported personal roles and result or actual procedural position. These fields state the work directly; they do not narrate what a source document says or assess its completeness.
The internal Audit evaluates that same case: verdict and target, ordered portfolio and hero, individual strategy, material reserve comparisons and useful remaining actions. Its evaluative voice belongs in the Audit. Do not copy internal evidence-gap caveats, research limitations or nomination advice into Submission prose. An accurate pending status belongs in the Submission; a request to provide missing proof belongs in the Audit.

Preservation with field-specific confidentiality
Preserve decisive legal mechanisms, outcomes, scale and attribution supported by the original sources. Preserve economic concepts and currencies separately. Do not infer cross-border legal work from a foreign parent. Confidential matter narratives can retain authorized private details in section E. Public B9/B10/C2 must preserve the legal contribution while generalizing restricted names and identifying combinations. An instruction to retain evidence never authorizes a public disclosure. Prior employers, associations and credentials retain their actual relationship and name unless an applicable restriction exists.

Proportionate development
Develop the evidence according to the task's depth policy. Do not impose a universal word count, paragraph count or expansion for its own sake. A richer source can support fuller narrative; a short source cannot justify invention or repetition. The B9 depth policy and the executive Audit length policy govern different outputs. Neither overrides the other. A repair fixes its assigned defects and preserves valid content rather than starting a new strategy.

Comparative reasoning and limits
Compare core and reserve matters by their incremental legal contribution, outcomes, capability coverage and supported personal roles. Distinguish portfolio selection from personal evidence eligibility under the task's rules. Do not equate absence of submitted evidence with absence of capability. Use external market benchmarks only when actually supplied and appropriately scoped; otherwise explain the evidence-based recommendation and its limits without inventing competitors or bands. Follow up only genuine missing facts whose answers could change a decision. Optional follow-up and stylistic preferences are not reasons to withhold an accurate available document.
