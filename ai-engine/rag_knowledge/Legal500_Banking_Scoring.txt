# Legal 500 Banking Scoring Rubric

## Weighting priorities

### Breadth of practice — 20
### Client service / responsiveness — 20
### Consistency of deal flow — 15
### Team depth — 15
### Commerciality — 10
### Sector coverage — 10
### Complexity / sophistication — 10

## Key insight

Legal 500 rewards sustainable market presence and strong commercial relationships as much as technical sophistication.
