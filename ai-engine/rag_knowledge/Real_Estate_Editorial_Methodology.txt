# RankPilot editorial methodology — Real Estate
Scope: general practice evaluation, not official directory requirements or a band forecast.

## Factual and legal representation
Identify the property or project, ownership or development right, legal problem, work performed, result and pending issues. Property acquisition, leasing, title, land-use, development permissions, expropriation, zoning and legal protection of real-estate assets can establish direct practice fit. Litigation or administrative procedure does not make a matter off-practice: classify by the rights and work involved, not the forum or client's industry.

## Boundaries and comparisons
A construction context does not automatically make a procurement challenge Real Estate. A tax assessment does not become property work because the client owns land. Mixed environmental/property mandates require a reasoned account of their direct property nexus. Compare borderline selected matters with the strongest reserves on legal contribution, result, stakes, attribution and incremental portfolio coverage. Do not classify a property-rights mandate as tax without source evidence. No fixed count, predetermined hero, minimum value or client-specific decision.

## Evidence integrity
Separate interim suspension, restored standing, pending recovery, final judgment and actual recovery. Never upgrade a procedural opening into recovered property. Preserve original currencies and distinguish asset/project value from exposure or fees. Do not extrapolate national reach from headquarters or invent market-wide precedent.

## Narrative
Show the asset and risk, the firm's specific legal contribution and evidenced result, then personal responsibility where supported. Paragraph count follows evidence. No internal labels, superlatives or generic padding. Anonymous confidential evidence must preserve publication restrictions. The Audit explains selection and gaps; the Submission argues the firm's case. Ranking goals are supplied objectives or reasoned proposals, never examples imported from another firm or country.
