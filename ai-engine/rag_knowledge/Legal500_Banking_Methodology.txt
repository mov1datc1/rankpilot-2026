# Legal 500 Banking Methodology — RankPilot RAG v1

## Editorial philosophy

Legal 500 evaluates Banking & Finance with strong emphasis on:
- breadth of capability
- consistency
- client service
- commerciality
- sector coverage
- depth of team
- ability to handle mainstream financing work

The directory values both sophistication and sustained market presence.

## Core Banking scope

Includes:
- acquisition finance
- asset finance
- structured finance
- trade finance
- financial regulatory work
- banking litigation
- mainstream financing
- lending and borrowing mandates

## What Legal 500 rewards

- broad financing capability
- strong client relationships
- responsiveness
- consistent deal flow
- sector diversity
- practical legal advice
- full-service support

## RankPilot instruction

For Legal 500, RankPilot should evaluate:
- Does the submission show a rounded practice?
- Does the team appear commercially strong?
- Is there evidence of consistent activity?
- Is client service visible?
