# Leaders League Banking Methodology — RankPilot RAG v1

## Editorial philosophy

Leaders League places strong emphasis on:
- market reputation
- visibility
- client prestige
- peer perception
- strategic positioning
- differentiation
- quality and complexity of matters

Its methodology relies heavily on:
- submission forms
- interviews
- client feedback
- peer feedback
- third-party market review

## Core evaluation criteria

- nature and complexity of matters
- size and volume of matters
- innovation
- recognition in the market
- partner reputation
- client diversification
- internationalisation
- growth of the practice

## RankPilot instruction

For Leaders League, RankPilot should ask:
- Does the submission feel market-leading?
- Is the practice differentiated?
- Is the firm visible and recognisable?
- Does the client base convey prestige?
