# IFLR1000 Banking Methodology — RankPilot RAG v1

## Editorial philosophy

IFLR1000 evaluates Banking & Finance through a highly technical and transaction-driven lens.

The ranking system places strong emphasis on:
- financial sophistication
- structuring complexity
- innovation
- technically demanding financing structures
- market-leading transactions
- cross-border finance
- lender sophistication
- deal engineering

Unlike Chambers, IFLR is less narrative-driven and more transaction-architecture driven.

## Core Banking scope

Relevant work includes:
- acquisition financing
- corporate financing
- mezzanine financing
- refinancing
- revolving credit facilities
- real estate finance
- asset finance
- trade finance
- structured lending
- leveraged finance

## High-ranking signals

IFLR strongly rewards:
- novel financing structures
- multi-layer debt architecture
- structured products
- complex collateral packages
- hybrid financing
- securitisation crossover
- cross-border lender coordination
- high-yield integration
- alternative credit structures

## Weak signals

IFLR gives less weight to:
- routine bilateral lending
- generic refinancing
- volume without sophistication
- purely local execution
- low-structure transactions

## RankPilot instruction

For IFLR, RankPilot must ask:
- What is technically sophisticated?
- What was financially innovative?
- What structure differentiates the matter?
- What legal-financial engineering was required?
