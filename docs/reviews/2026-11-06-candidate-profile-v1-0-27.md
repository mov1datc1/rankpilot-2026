# Source profile evidence and category decisions v1.0.27

The last acceptance revealed three gaps: source career credentials disappeared when mandate-based B9 prose was generated, a generic Band ranking candidate label passed the category validator, and identity anonymization could turn a previous employer into a client.

Development v4 registers source profile units separately from matter evidence. Each candidate accounts for every supplied unit with its original quotation, retention/generalization/omission decision, public quotation and internal reason. Explicit career and professional-role evidence cannot simply be omitted; confidential identities may be generalized while preserving roles and relationships. Missing, duplicate, foreign or unexecuted profile evidence routes through the existing candidate-only repair, preserving source, portfolio, other candidates and budgets. The independent reviewer receives a field-bound obligation for each original public profile unit and judges its material execution rather than trusting the generated retention decision. Unpublished Legal 500 roster entries are not treated as missing public biographies.

Generic category placeholders are rejected. An unresolved category must be Category pending with a specific undecided criterion and an actionable next step; the pending label stays internal. Concrete proposals remain targets, never assertions of recognition. The writer and judge must reconcile the same category and reason in the Audit. Chambers guidance distinguishes growth/maturity-based Up-and-Coming from associate-specific categories; no person or firm is assigned a fixed category by name. Sources: https://chambers.com/submissions/rankings-explained and https://chambers.com/submissions/methodology (read 6 November 2026).

The anonymizer uses a relationship-neutral identity replacement unless the sentence explicitly establishes a firm/client relationship. It preserves career-role syntax and no longer infers client status solely from an entity's presence in the confidential register. Final proof quotes for v4 use the actual public profile rows extracted from the Word after rendering, including anonymization and formatting; a stale generated bio cannot substitute for that field. Existing whole-document and own-source checks remain mandatory.

Historical development v1–v3 remains readable. The v4 development/checkpoint contract, renderer 21 and artifact-review 21 prevent a previous approval from silently certifying the new output. No new model role, budget extension, source modification or automatic restart of failed attempts is introduced.

Validation: 514 Python tests and 482 Node passes/one skip, including full Word profile extraction, previous-employer and association cases, omission/foreign-quote rejection, category/pending decisions, scoped repair and legacy compatibility. TypeScript passes. Read-only private replays detect the old generic categories and missing profile accountability and preserve the previous-employer role in the rebuilt Word without model calls. These are regression checks, not a new autonomous production acceptance or a guarantee of every future editorial judgment.
