DeForest Abogados advises employers on Labour &amp; Employment matters where workforce stability, regulatory compliance and operational continuity are closely connected. The practice combines employment advisory, labour litigation, collective labour relations, social security, compliance and workforce governance for domestic and multinational businesses operating throughout Mexico.

The team advises across the employment lifecycle, including hiring, day-to-day advisory, collective bargaining, restructurings, labour inspections, social-security matters, constitutional labour litigation and workforce integration following corporate transactions. Its work includes collective-bargaining negotiations and union-representation disputes, nationwide litigation portfolios, post-acquisition employment integration, specialised-services and REPSE projects, and labour governance for infrastructure and industrial operations. During the last twelve months, the team assisted more than 20 clients in connection with Ministry of Labor inspections and handled numerous labour and social-security compliance audits. The practice advises employers whose combined workforce exceeds one million employees throughout Mexico and includes approximately 700 labour disputes managed by the firm’s Puebla team.

Key partners include Eduardo Garduño, Jaime Bustamante, Javier Atzin Vallejo and Raymundo Carreño. Garduño leads employment integration, collective-labour and workforce-governance mandates for industrial employers. Bustamante combines complex employment disputes, workplace investigations, compliance and preventive governance. Vallejo leads labour and social-security work for infrastructure and manufacturing operations, including collective-labour disputes and constitutional proceedings. Carreño contributes longstanding corporate labour-relations experience developed in the automotive sector.

The wider leadership group includes Andrés Cabrera Gómez in the Bajío region, together with lawyers focused on litigation, social security, labour compliance and workforce implementation. The practice advises employers across labour-intensive and regulated industries, including manufacturing, automotive, infrastructure, energy, logistics, technology, telecommunications, retail and financial services.
