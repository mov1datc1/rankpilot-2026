# Source-bound selection review scope v1.0.35

After a local repair, the next reviewer re-examined every previously accepted decision. A corrected inclusion could therefore expose unrelated new follow-up requests and exhaust the existing cap before drafting. Repeated full judgments were inconsistent with preserving unaffected decisions.

Passing checks now carry a fingerprint of the exact original source, current decision, practice scope, delivery policy, leadership input, thesis, Hero and declared comparison dependencies. The next review targets only checks without a matching proof. A changed source or decision reopens its own check; changed dependencies reopen related comparisons. An undeclared comparative premise conservatively depends on the complete portfolio. Legacy diagnostics have no implicit proof and receive the ordinary full review. Out-of-scope new check keys fail the transport schema.

This reuses a recorded independent judgment; it does not turn a rejection into a pass, amend a source, grant more attempts or approve documents. Remaining rejected checks still require bounded correction and independent review. The final exact-Word reviewer continues to inspect the complete original evidence and both documents.

The reviewer can separately record source-bound, nonblocking advisories for optional follow-up that does not affect the selected decision. A missing decisive fact, invented financial meaning or false result remains a blocking correction. Advisories are passed to the Audit writer as optional follow-up, without becoming filing prerequisites. Unbound advisory text is neither retained as evidence nor cached.

Validation: 558 Python tests, including actual graph/checkpoint deferral accounting, unchanged-proof reuse, source/scope/privacy changes, declared and undeclared comparison dependencies, strict transport scope, legacy behavior, advisory grounding and unchanged retry caps. Node contracts and TypeScript remain required release checks. A separate offline replay uses existing responses without buying another judgment; it is not fresh autonomous or browser acceptance. Private sources, responses and documents are excluded from this release.
