Client: GRUPO R -CONCESIÓN- ZAPOPAN. GRUPO R began its operations in the area of fuel service stations and has recently expanded into the energy sector as a public lighting operator and clean energy generator for the National Electric System, making it one of the main energy companies in the State of Jalisco.
GRUPO R was awarded the Public Lighting Concession of Zapopan in the State of Jalisco, with an approximate value of more than 4 billion mexican pesos for a term of 18 years, after participating in a public bidding process. Given the magnitude of the matter, the non-winning bidders and third parties filed multiple lawsuits to paralyze and revoke the concession. Approximately 35 lawsuits were filed to challenge the concession. In these lawsuits, the plaintiffs argued that the bidding process should have been awarded to them because GRUPO R allegedly did not comply with the legal requirements set forth in the bidding conditions, or because their technical proposal (for lighting fixtures or the provision of public lighting services), as well as their economic proposal, was allegedly better than that of our client. Thanks to our firm's legal advice, we have obtained more than 30 favourable resolutions for our client, denying lawsuits that were promoted against the awarding of the concession. We were able to establish very important legal criteria, such as: i) that the effects of the concession and the concession contract signed cannot be paralyzed, since it has public order and social interest purposes; ii) that third parties who did not participate in the bidding process consented to the bases of the procedure, so they cannot claim issues inherent to it; and above all, iii) it was proven that the award to our client was correct, since it was the best proposal. To date, only 3 lawsuits are still pending, the rest were favorably resolved in favor of the company, thanks to the work of the firm. This case is highly relevant because it aims to protect a concession that has social utility, public order and social interest purposes; besides allowing the company to position itself as a regional leader in public lighting thanks to its commitment to innovation, among other elements.

IDEX - Brasilia. Idex is an innovative company in the construction industry, leader in the State of Jalisco, with 45 years of experience in the area.
D2 Summary of matter and your department's role – Please say why this matter was important. Also, tell us exactly what role your department played.
IDEX, among its various projects, initiated the commercially denominated "Brasilia", located in the city of Guadalajara, Jalisco, consisting of a vertical development of 328 housing units and 20 commercial units. This development was the object of a serious attack by neighbors, neighborhood associations and the State Urban Development Attorney's Office (PRODEUR), who launched media, legal and jurisdictional attacks, alleging that "Brasilia" affected the correct urban development of the city, since it allegedly had a negative impact on traffic, concentration of inhabitants, water and electricity services and the enjoyment of the city, in addition to claiming alleged damage to groundwater. The aforementioned, without having any real legal basis, but simply the pretension of obtaining benefits through collective pressure. These organizations sought the closure, obstruction and non-execution of Brasilia by filing several injunction lawsuits, requesting suspensions and exerting pressure through the authorities. As a result, the development was the victim of four simultaneous suspensions that ordered the closure of the development, based on alleged environmental impacts and risks. These suspensions were the result of four different lawsuits that ordered the closure of the project to prevent its execution. Our team of lawyers managed to revoke the four suspensions in a period of 3 weeks, generating important legal criteria, such as: (i) the environmental impact must be sufficiently accredited as something real, actual and imminent and not only as a mere assumption without basis; (ii) although there is a Supreme Court criterion to immediately halt developments when an environmental impact is alleged, we achieved its application in a balanced manner, leaving established that whoever claims an environmental impact or risk must do so with sufficient grounds and not based on allegations without technical support. Our firm was able to establish a very important legal precedent, even though the Supreme Court of Justice of Mexico ruled against it. As a result, developers now have guarantees regarding the continuity of their operations. This case evidenced a clear opportunity to defend against generic or unfounded allegations of environmental damage.

This matter demonstrates that the environment is a sensitive issue and environmental measures must be technically based on scientific studies. D2 Summary of matter and your department's role – Please say why this matter was important. Also, tell us exactly what role your department played.
INMOBILIARIA DESARROLLO LA PRIMAVERA, S.A. de C.V., is a company dedicated to purchasing and administering real estate and personal property, particularly for housing-project construction in Jalisco. On 28 February 2017, the Official Gazette of the State of Jalisco published the “Agreement of the Citizen Constitutional Governor of the State,” ordering the administrative recovery of various properties located in the tributaries of Avenida Acueducto, Anillo Periférico and Avenida de la Patria, Zapopan, Jalisco. The agreement designated the properties for public service—specifically, Guadalajara’s drinking-water supply—and provided that they would become state property in the public domain and for common use, free of physical barriers restricting access, enjoyment and use. One of the properties subject to administrative recovery belonged to the client; its possession was taken and it was used for purposes other than those identified in the agreement. The matter has an approximate value of MXN 100,000,000.00 (USD 5,536,728.22).
The firm initiated a contingency plan comprising various legal actions, including amparo proceeding 932/2017 before the Second District Court in Administrative, Civil and Labor Matters in the State of Jalisco.
