Ramos Castillo Abogados is known for strategically focused Real Estate counsel where property rights, development viability and regulatory compliance are commercially inseparable. The team is instructed on mandates requiring the coordinated application of transactional, regulatory and contentious real estate expertise, particularly where legal uncertainty threatens the continuity, value or implementability of an investment. Jose Pablo Ramos Castillo provides strategic leadership on the practice’s most consequential matters, combining senior-level direction with close attention to the legal issues capable of affecting an asset’s commercial outcome. The department’s operating model is deliberately used: senior partners retain direct involvement in mandates requiring strategic judgement, while specialised associates contribute substantive technical execution and continuity throughout the life of the instruction. This structure enables the team to maintain partner-led command over material decisions without sacrificing the detailed analysis and disciplined implementation required in sophisticated real estate matters.

The practice acts for domestic conglomerates, financial institutions and multinational corporations, reflecting its capacity to advise clients whose real estate interests must withstand demanding commercial and regulatory scrutiny. It is not defined merely by a local property-law remit; rather, it addresses real estate mandates across the jurisdiction through an integrated approach to property, development, regulatory and dispute-related risk. Its standing rests on the ability to translate complex legal questions into practical measures that safeguard assets, support compliant development and preserve investment continuity when the legal status of real estate is commercially critical.

D2 Summary of matter and your department's role – Please say why this matter was important. Also, tell us exactly what role your department played.
IDEX’s Brasilia vertical development in Guadalajara, Jalisco—comprising 328 housing units and 20 commercial units, with a value of MXN 1,300,000,000.00 (approx. USD 74,747,252.26)—faced an immediate threat to its construction, sale and delivery timetable. Neighbours, neighbourhood associations and PRODEUR pursued media, administrative and judicial challenges alleging traffic, population-density, utility-service, urban-enjoyment and groundwater impacts, resulting in 4 simultaneous suspensions ordering the project’s closure and preventing its execution.

José Pablo Ramos Castillo and the team responded by challenging the evidential basis for the alleged environmental risks and securing revocation of all 4 suspensions within 3 weeks. Won in early 2020, the matter resulted in the dismissal of the 4 lawsuits and suspensions, confirming the full legality of Brasilia and allowing its construction, sale and delivery to proceed according to schedule. The outcome provided developers with a materially stronger basis to resist generic or unsubstantiated environmental allegations deployed to obstruct lawful projects. José Pablo Ramos Castillo led the representation, supported by Daniel Rocha Peña and Héctor Alejandro Sánchez Carrera.
