EL CIELO COUNTRY CLUB — a high-end residential development at Cerro de Bugambilias combining urban development with environmental conservation.
D2 Summary of matter and your department's role – Please say why this matter was important. Also, tell us exactly what role your department played.
El Cielo Country Club is an established, high-end residential community on Cerro de Bugambilias, south of Guadalajara, combining homes and urban infrastructure with extensive conserved land. Successive state and municipal measures sought to reclassify the development as a protected natural area and apply a new ecological programme to land that had already been authorised and sold. The measures placed valid permits, purchasers' acquired rights and an approximately MXN 3 billion (approximately USD 176.6 million) development at existential risk.

Ramos Castillo prevented public authorities from rewriting the legal history of an operating development. The firm turned vested rights - often treated as an abstract constitutional concept - into the instrument that kept the project commercially alive. The team designed and led two amparo proceedings, assembled the scientific and technical record required to defeat allegations of environmental harm, and proved that later political measures could not extinguish duly acquired development rights. It secured a judgment confirming the legality of the project and validity of its permits, upheld by the Sixth Collegiate Administrative Court (case 347/2022); it then obtained a second judgment disapplying the updated ecological management decree and achieved full judicial enforcement in July 2024.

José Pablo Ramos Castillo led the strategy, supported by Edgar Adrián Moro López and Mónica Dariane Cárdenas Fregoso. Their work preserved the development, protected existing purchasers and established a powerful proposition for the wider market: environmental regulation can shape future development, but it cannot arbitrarily erase lawful investment already made in reliance on government authorisations.

José Pablo Ramos Castillo led the mandate alongside senior associates Edgar Adrián Moro López and Mónica Dariane Cárdenas Fregoso. Their intervention protected far more than acreage: it preserved the foundation asset, the client's negotiating position and years of industrial-project planning. D2 Summary of matter and your department's role – Please say why this matter was important. Also, tell us exactly what role your department played.
On February 28, 2017, was published in the Official Gazette of the State of Jalisco the "Agreement of the Citizen Constitutional Governor of the State", ordering the administrative recovery of various properties or real estate located in the tributaries of the roads, Avenida Acueducto, Anillo Periférico and Avenida de la Patria, in the Municipality of Zapopan, of the State of Jalisco. In accordance with the Agreement, the properties are destined to a public service, and in this particular case, to the supply of drinking water to the city of Guadalajara. This decree of administrative recovery implies that such properties become state property, of public domain and of common use. In this sense, these properties should be free of all physical barriers that restrict the access, enjoyment and use of the same, according to the terms of the Agreement. One of the properties subject to administrative recovery belongs to our client: INMOBILIARIA DESARROLLO LA PRIMAVERA. In an unjustified manner, the possession of its property was taken away and disposed of for purposes that were not those indicated in the Administrative Agreement. Therefore, our firm initiated a contingency plan with various legal actions, among which the amparo lawsuit 932/2017 before the Second District Court in Administrative, Civil and Labor Matters in the State of Jalisco stood out, where we were able to demonstrate, through expert evidence on real estate identification, that the properties acquired by the Government of the State of Jalisco are not located in the place or area stipulated in the Agreement and that, therefore, they had unjustifiably invaded our client's private property. Therefore, our client acquired legal standing to request compensation for expropriation or to demand the restitution of the property.
