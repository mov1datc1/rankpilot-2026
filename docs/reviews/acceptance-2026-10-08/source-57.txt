Our Firm’s tax practice is well known for its expertise on a broad range of tax issues including consulting and general advising on tax matters and the interrelations with corporate, regulatory, labor and finance issues.
Our team has the capability to face any complexities and work with the clients, managing the important changes and challenges that Venezuela imposes due to the political and economic environment.

Over the last year, our practice has encompassed tax planning of several Venezuelan and multinational entities, legal advice to individuals, inheritance, and wealth planning; as well as related tax disputes (administrative and judicial procedures against Tax authorities and Courts).

Moreover, our practice is recognized for providing timely and accurate assistance to clients in all tax matters involving their operations, including tax assistance in restructuring and reorganization projects. Our key clients include entities of the following economic sectors: oil and gas sector, banking and financial institutions, private medical services, construction, IT and software development, food and beverages production, information product and services, such as: Summus, Gruppo Montenegro; Editores Orientales, C.A (EOCA); Grupo BDO, SKU Logistics Corp, Microsoft Venezuela; PepsiCo Alimentos; Universal Music Venezuela; Grupo Pizzolante; Centro Médico de Caracas; Branza 1800, S.A.; Mapfre; Grupo Ceballos; Sucesión Zuloaga; Inversiones AEFEVE;.; Tecnipisicinas, S.A.; Grupo Otaola; Distribuidora Bolivar Films, Kyndryl de Venezuela, Industrias Venezolana de Limpieza, (VENELIN), Programa Mundial de Alimentos (UN).

We stand out among our peers for innovating in the sector and for our ability to adapt to change. The perfect example for that is ARAQUEREYNA WithU ( HYPERLINK "https://mailchi.mp/acc861a2cc48/emprendedores" https://mailchi.mp/acc861a2cc48/emprendedores), a project that offers legal kits, and preferential rates, to entrepreneurs; the purpose is that entrepreneurs have access to legal assistance for their new businesses in one of the most important law firms in Venezuela.

Developing a strong network of contacts within the most important practice related organizations and events in the tax field is one of our strong points. We act as an active member of international and local legal organizations, such as IFA, IBA, ABA, AVDT, Latin American Tax Association, VENAMCHAM, CAVECOL and BRITCHAM.

Thanks to our reputation on all Tax Law matters in the country, the firm has represented Venezuela in international tax events and forums during the last ten years, interacting with lawyers from different jurisdictions including USA, Asia, Europe, and Latin America, allowing the Firm to approach the tax practice area in a local and global way.

Over the past three years, we have worked hard to strengthen our department, bringing in both national and international cases of great scope, relevance and complexity.
