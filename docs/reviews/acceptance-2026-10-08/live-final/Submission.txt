B10 What is this department best known for?
Please include: industry sector expertise; key types of work; areas of recent growth.
Address any feedback on our recent coverage of your department (500 word count limit)
DeForest’s Labour &amp; Employment practice advises employers on workforce stability, collective labour relations, employment litigation, labour and social-security compliance, inspections and workforce governance across Mexico. The team combines contentious and non-contentious work for employers operating in labour-intensive and regulated sectors, including manufacturing, automotive, energy, infrastructure, technology, consumer services and financial services.

The practice is particularly active where employment strategy is closely connected to operational continuity. Its work includes post-acquisition labour integration, collective bargaining and union-representation disputes, prevention of work stoppages, nationwide labour litigation portfolios, workforce restructurings, employer substitutions, specialised-services compliance, internal investigations, employment documentation and social-security matters.

The team has advised on the harmonisation of labour structures for a workforce exceeding 5,000 employees across six Mexican states following an acquisition; collective labour disputes involving union representation and strike risk; nationwide litigation platforms comprising approximately 200 active proceedings; and employment-governance projects for industrial workforces of up to approximately 17,000 employees. Across its employer client base, the practice advises employers whose combined workforce exceeds one million employees throughout Mexico. Its Puebla team manages approximately 700 labour disputes, and during the last twelve months the team has assisted more than 20 clients in connection with labour inspections conducted by the Ministry of Labor.

It also advises on labour-reform implementation, REPSE-related reviews and supplier audits, labour and social-security inspections, and constitutional labour litigation. Eduardo Garduño leads the practice, with Javier Atzin Vallejo, Jaime Bustamante, José Alberto Díaz Méndez and other members of the team contributing across collective labour, workforce governance, litigation, compliance and social-security mandates. The practice is positioned to support employers throughout the employment lifecycle, from preventive governance and documentation to high-risk collective disputes and complex contentious matters.
