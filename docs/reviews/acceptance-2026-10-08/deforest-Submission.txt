DeForest’s Labour & Employment practice advises employers whose workforce stability is fundamental to business continuity. The team combines labour advisory, litigation, collective labour relations, social security, compliance and workforce governance for multinational and domestic companies operating throughout Mexico.

The practice supports the full employment lifecycle, from day-to-day advice and preventive documentation to collective bargaining, workforce restructurings, labour and social-security audits, inspections, constitutional proceedings and litigation portfolios. Its work is concentrated in labour-intensive and regulated sectors, including automotive and advanced manufacturing, infrastructure, energy, logistics, retail, technology, telecommunications and financial services.

The department is led by Eduardo Garduño, with Javier Atzin Vallejo and Jaime Bustamante as Partners. The wider team includes José Alberto Díaz Méndez, Andrés Cabrera Gómez, Raymundo Carreño, Edgar Barreto and Erick Pérez. Their combined experience supports collective labour strategy, compliance, social security, litigation and regional implementation.

Representative work includes post-acquisition labour integration across six Mexican states for more than 5,000 employees; collective bargaining and union-representation matters involving strike-prevention strategy; nationwide litigation portfolios; workforce restructurings; and labour and social-security governance for industrial operations. The practice also manages approximately 700 labour disputes through its Puebla team and has assisted more than 20 clients in Ministry of Labor inspections during the last 12 months.
