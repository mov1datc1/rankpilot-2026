Ramos Castillo protects the business value of real estate assets when regulatory intervention, environmental measures, expropriation or litigation threaten to halt a development, deprive an owner of its land or render an investment commercially unviable. José Pablo’s strategic leadership is supported by Edgar Adrián Moro López and Mónica Dariane Cárdenas Fregoso. Client: GRUPO R -CONCESIÓN- ZAPOPAN. GRUPO R began its operations in the area of fuel service stations and has recently expanded into the energy sector as a public lighting operator and clean energy generator for the National Electric System, making it one of the main energy companies in the State of Jalisco.
GRUPO R was awarded the Public Lighting Concession of Zapopan in the State of Jalisco, with an approximate value of more than 4 billion mexican pesos for a term of 18 years, after participating in a public bidding process. Given the magnitude of the matter, the non-winning bidders and third parties filed multiple lawsuits to paralyze and revoke the concession. Approximately 35 lawsuits were filed to challenge the concession. In these lawsuits, the plaintiffs argued that the bidding process should have been awarded to them because GRUPO R allegedly did not comply with the legal requirements set forth in the bidding conditions, or because their technical proposal (for lighting fixtures or the provision of public lighting services), as well as their economic proposal, was allegedly better than that of our client. Thanks to our firm's legal advice, we have obtained more than 30 favourable resolutions for our client, denying lawsuits that were promoted against the awarding of the concession. We were able to establish very important legal criteria, such as: i) that the effects of the concession and the concession contract signed cannot be paralyzed, since it has public order and social interest purposes; ii) that third parties who did not participate in the bidding process consented to the bases of the procedure, so they cannot claim issues inherent to it; and above all, iii) it was proven that the award to our client was correct, since it was the best proposal. To date, only 3 lawsuits are still pending, the rest were favorably resolved in favor of the company, thanks to the work of the firm. This case is highly relevant because it aims to protect a concession that has social utility, public order and social interest purposes; besides allowing the company to position itself as a regional leader in public lighting thanks to its commitment to innovation, among other elements.

IDEX - Brasilia, a construction-industry company described as a leader in Jalisco with 45 years of experience, initiated the commercially denominated Brasilia project in Guadalajara, Jalisco. The MXN 1.300.000.000,00 development (approximately USD 74,747,252.26) comprises a vertical development of 328 housing units and 20 commercial units. Neighbours, neighbourhood associations and the State Urban Development Attorney’s Office (PRODEUR) launched media, legal and jurisdictional attacks, alleging adverse effects on urban development, traffic, population concentration, water and electricity services, enjoyment of the city and groundwater; they sought the project’s closure, obstruction and non-execution through several injunction lawsuits, requests for suspensions and pressure through authorities.
Four simultaneous suspensions arising from four different lawsuits ordered the project’s closure on alleged environmental impacts and risks. The team revoked all four suspensions within three weeks, establishing that environmental impact must be shown to be real, actual and imminent rather than an unsupported assumption, and achieving a balanced application of a Supreme Court criterion requiring the immediate halt of developments when environmental impact is alleged. The matter was won in early 2020, with the four lawsuits and suspensions successfully dismissed, confirming the development’s full legality and allowing its construction, sale and delivery according to schedule.
José Pablo Ramos Castillo led the matter, with Daniel Peña Rocha and Hector Alejandro Sanchez Carrera on the team; no other firms were involved. The firm established what it described as an important legal precedent, notwithstanding that the Supreme Court of Justice of Mexico ruled against it, and the matter stated that developers now have guarantees regarding operational continuity and a clear opportunity to defend against generic or unfounded allegations of environmental damage.

EL CIELO COUNTRY CLUB. (https://elcielocc.com/) A first class development located in the Bugambilias hill, where urbanity is combined with respect and coexistence with nature, making it one of the most attractive developments in the State of Jalisco.
D2 Summary of matter and your department's role – Please say why this matter was important. Also, tell us exactly what role your department played.
The Government of the State of Jalisco and the Municipality of Tlajomulco (in the same State), issued administrative Decrees, by means of which it was determined that El Cielo Country Club represents a Natural Protected Area. As a consequence of these Decrees, the urbanisation process of said area was stopped. The aforementioned implied a very serious impact on the property and a serious risk to the continuity and subsistence of the development, as well as to the property of the purchasers. Against such decrees, our team of lawyers filed two amparo lawsuits, one in 2018 and the other in 2021. In the first one, our firm achieved a very important resolution in the first instance, where it was recognized: i) the legality of the development; ii) the validity of the permits for a housing development granted prior to the Decrees that intended to disqualify the development; and above all, iii) that the development does not affect the environment, but on the contrary, it is environmentally friendly, sustainable and combines urbanisation with respect and coexistence with nature. This criterion is of great importance, not only for the company that maintains the development, its patrimony, its image and subsistence, but also for third parties. At the legal level, the work of our firm was key to set a very important precedent. In this way, it is established that it is not possible for political decisions to disregard land uses already granted and in execution, since the authority is obliged to respect the authorizations issued, which generates legal certainty for investment in urban development. This matter demonstrates that the environment is a sensitive issue and environmental measures must be technically based on scientific studies. The Amparo filed by our firm in 2021, is in the stage of integration of expert evidence, awaiting resolution in early 2024.
