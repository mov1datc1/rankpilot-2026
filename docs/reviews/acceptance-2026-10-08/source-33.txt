Publishable Matter 1
D1 Name of client – this will be publishable. If you cannot reveal the client name, give a general description.
GRUPO R -CONCESIÓN- ZAPOPAN. GRUPO R began its operations in the area of fuel service stations and has recently expanded into the energy sector as a public lighting operator and clean energy generator for the National Electric System, making it one of the main energy companies in the State of Jalisco.
D2 Summary of matter and your department's role – Please say why this matter was important. Also, tell us exactly what role your department played.
GRUPO R was awarded the Public Lighting Concession of Zapopan in the State of Jalisco, with an approximate value of more than 4 billion mexican pesos for a term of 18 years, after participating in a public bidding process.
Given the magnitude of the matter, the non-winning bidders and third parties filed multiple lawsuits to paralyze and revoke the concession. Approximately 35 lawsuits were filed to challenge the concession.
In these lawsuits, the plaintiffs argued that the bidding process should have been awarded to them because GRUPO R allegedly did not comply with the legal requirements set forth in the bidding conditions, or because their technical proposal (for lighting fixtures or the provision of public lighting services), as well as their economic proposal, was allegedly better than that of our client.
Thanks to our firm's legal advice, we have obtained more than 30 favourable resolutions for our client, denying lawsuits that were promoted against the awarding of the concession.
We were able to establish very important legal criteria, such as: i) that the effects of the concession and the concession contract signed cannot be paralyzed, since it has public order and social interest purposes; ii) that third parties who did not participate in the bidding process consented to the bases of the procedure, so they cannot claim issues inherent to it; and above all, iii) it was proven that the award to our client was correct, since it was the best proposal.
To date, only 3 lawsuits are still pending, the rest were favorably resolved in favor of the company, thanks to the work of the firm.
This case is highly relevant because it aims to protect a concession that has social utility, public order and social interest purposes; besides allowing the company to position itself as a regional leader in public lighting thanks to its commitment to innovation, among other elements.

Publishable Matter 2
D1 Name of client – this will be publishable. If you cannot reveal the client name, give a general description.
IDEX - Brasilia. Idex is an innovative company in the construction industry, leader in the State of Jalisco, with 45 years of experience in the area.
D2 Summary of matter and your department's role – Please say why this matter was important. Also, tell us exactly what role your department played.
IDEX, among its various projects, initiated the commercially denominated "Brasilia", located in the city of Guadalajara, Jalisco, consisting of a vertical development of 328 housing units and 20 commercial units.
This development was the object of a serious attack by neighbors, neighborhood associations and the State Urban Development Attorney's Office (PRODEUR), who launched media, legal and jurisdictional attacks, alleging that "Brasilia" affected the correct urban development of the city, since it allegedly had a negative impact on traffic, concentration of inhabitants, water and electricity services and the enjoyment of the city, in addition to claiming alleged damage to groundwater.
The aforementioned, without having any real legal basis, but simply the pretension of obtaining benefits through collective pressure.
These organizations sought the closure, obstruction and non-execution of Brasilia by filing several injunction lawsuits, requesting suspensions and exerting pressure through the authorities.
As a result, the development was the victim of four simultaneous suspensions that ordered the closure of the development, based on alleged environmental impacts and risks. These suspensions were the result of four different lawsuits that ordered the closure of the project to prevent its execution.
Our team of lawyers managed to revoke the four suspensions in a period of 3 weeks, generating important legal criteria, such as: (i) the environmental impact must be sufficiently accredited as something real, actual and imminent and not only as a mere assumption without basis; (ii) although there is a Supreme Court criterion to immediately halt developments when an environmental impact is alleged, we achieved its application in a balanced manner, leaving established that whoever claims an environmental impact or risk must do so with sufficient grounds and not based on allegations without technical support.
Our firm was able to establish a very important legal precedent, even though the Supreme Court of Justice of Mexico ruled against it. As a result, developers now have guarantees regarding the continuity of their operations. This case evidenced a clear opportunity to defend against generic or unfounded allegations of environmental damage.

EL CIELO COUNTRY CLUB. (https://elcielocc.com/) A first class development located in the Bugambilias hill, where urbanity is combined with respect and coexistence with nature, making it one of the most attractive developments in the State of Jalisco.
D2 Summary of matter and your department's role – Please say why this matter was important. Also, tell us exactly what role your department played.
The Government of the State of Jalisco and the Municipality of Tlajomulco (in the same State), issued administrative Decrees, by means of which it was determined that El Cielo Country Club represents a Natural Protected Area. As a consequence of these Decrees, the urbanisation process of said area was stopped.
The aforementioned implied a very serious impact on the property and a serious risk to the continuity and subsistence of the development, as well as to the property of the purchasers.
Against such decrees, our team of lawyers filed two amparo lawsuits, one in 2018 and the other in 2021.
In the first one, our firm achieved a very important resolution in the first instance, where it was recognized: i) the legality of the development; ii) the validity of the permits for a housing development granted prior to the Decrees that intended to disqualify the development; and above all, iii) that the development does not affect the environment, but on the contrary, it is environmentally friendly, sustainable and combines urbanisation with respect and coexistence with nature.
This criterion is of great importance, not only for the company that maintains the development, its patrimony, its image and subsistence, but also for third parties. At the legal level, the work of our firm was key to set a very important precedent.
In this way, it is established that it is not possible for political decisions to disregard land uses already granted and in execution, since the authority is obliged to respect the authorizations issued, which generates legal certainty for investment in urban development.
 This matter demonstrates that the environment is a sensitive issue and environmental measures must be technically based on scientific studies.
The Amparo filed by our firm in 2021, is in the stage of integration of expert evidence, awaiting resolution in early 2024.

Publishable Matter 7
D1 Name of client – this will be publishable. If you cannot reveal the client name, give a general description.
INMOBILIARIA DESARROLLO LA PRIMAVERA. S.A. de C.V. Company dedicated to the purchase and administration of real estate and personal property, particularly oriented to the construction of housing projects in the State of Jalisco.
D2 Summary of matter and your department's role – Please say why this matter was important. Also, tell us exactly what role your department played.
On February 28, 2017, was published in the Official Gazette of the State of Jalisco the "Agreement of the Citizen Constitutional Governor of the State", ordering the administrative recovery of various properties or real estate located in the tributaries of the roads, Avenida Acueducto, Anillo Periférico and Avenida de la Patria, in the Municipality of Zapopan, of the State of Jalisco. In accordance with the Agreement, the properties are destined to a public service, and in this particular case, to the supply of drinking water to the city of Guadalajara.
This decree of administrative recovery implies that such properties become state property, of public domain and of common use. In this sense, these properties should be free of all physical barriers that restrict the access, enjoyment and use of the same, according to the terms of the Agreement.
One of the properties subject to administrative recovery belongs to our client: INMOBILIARIA DESARROLLO LA PRIMAVERA. In an unjustified manner, the possession of its property was taken away and disposed of for purposes that were not those indicated in the Administrative Agreement.
Therefore, our firm initiated a contingency plan with various legal actions, among which the amparo lawsuit 932/2017 before the Second District Court in Administrative, Civil and Labor Matters in the State of Jalisco stood out, where we were able to demonstrate, through expert evidence on real estate identification, that the properties acquired by the Government of the State of Jalisco are not located in the place or area stipulated in the Agreement and that, therefore, they had unjustifiably invaded our client's private property.
