ARAQUEREYNA’s Tax practice advises on Venezuelan tax matters across transactions, controversy, operational taxation, corporate reorganisations and private-wealth planning. The team’s work includes direct and indirect taxes, income tax, VAT, municipal taxation, customs-related tax issues, transfer pricing, advance-payment regimes, tax recovery regimes, inheritance tax and tax planning.

The practice regularly combines tax advice with corporate, M&A, labour, regulatory and finance capabilities where mandates require an integrated response. Recent work includes tax advice on Gruppo Montenegro’s acquisition of the Pampero rum brand and the Venezuelan framework for its operation and distribution; assistance to PEPSICO in SENIAT audit procedures concerning income tax and VAT, foreign-exchange gains and losses, and transfer pricing; and tax assistance to Turkish Airlines in a Ministry of Tourism audit concerning the special tourism tax.

The team also advises clients on national and municipal tax obligations, authority-facing requests and consultations, corporate restructurings, market-entry structures and cross-border wealth and succession planning. Its private-client work includes planning for family groups, inherited estates and Venezuelan real-estate holding structures, including mandates with connections to Spain, Barbados and the United States.

The department is led by senior practitioners María Carolina Cano, Gabriel Ruán Santos and Ingrid García Pacheco. María Carolina Cano leads transactional, audit, structuring and wealth-planning mandates; Gabriel Ruán Santos contributes experience in contentious tax work and national-tax advisory matters; and Ingrid García Pacheco leads matters involving local taxation, estate planning and international structures. Senior Associate María Alejandra Garcia Nieto is listed across mandates involving tax audits, tax controversy, succession planning and tax structuring.
