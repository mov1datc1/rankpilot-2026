EL CIELO COUNTRY CLUB — A first class development located in the Bugambilias hill, where urbanity is combined with respect.
D2 Summary of matter and your department's role – Please say why this matter was important. Also, tell us exactly what role your department played.
El Cielo Country Club faced an existential threat to a MXN 3.000.000.000,00 development (approx. USD 172,37,026.00) in the Bugambilias hill, Jalisco, after the Government of the State of Jalisco and the Municipality of Tlajomulco designated the area as a Natural Protected Area. The decrees halted urbanisation, placing the continuity, patrimony and reputation of the development—and the property interests of its purchasers—at serious risk.

José Pablo Ramos Castillo’s team challenged the measures through amparo proceedings filed in 2018 and 2021, converting the client’s existing development permits and sustainability record into a strategy for protecting the project from retrospective administrative restrictions. In the 2018 proceeding, the team secured a first-instance ruling recognising the legality of the development, the continued validity of housing-development permits granted before the decrees, and the finding that the project did not harm the environment. The Sixth Collegiate Administrative Court of the Third Circuit, in case file 347/2022, subsequently upheld that amparo. In the 2021 proceeding, the team obtained annulment of the decree updating the Local Ecological Management Program for the Municipality of Tlajomulco de Zúñiga insofar as it applied to the client’s properties; the judgment was enforced in July 2024.

The outcome preserved the legal basis for El Cielo Country Club’s continued development and reinforced investor certainty for authorised urban projects confronted by subsequent environmental-designation measures. The rulings establish that authorities must respect land-use and development authorisations already granted and in execution, while grounding environmental restrictions in appropriate technical support. José Pablo Ramos Castillo led the matter, with Daniel Rocha Peña and Héctor Alejandro Sánchez Carrera acting on the team.
