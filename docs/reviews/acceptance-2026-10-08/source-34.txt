IDEX - Brasilia. Idex is an innovative company in the construction industry, leader in the State of Jalisco, with 45 years of experience in the area.
D2 Summary of matter and your department's role – Please say why this matter was important. Also, tell us exactly what role your department played.
In the Brasilia Vertical Development Defence, IDEX - Brasilia, associations and PRODEUR, were represented by the firm in urgent injunction proceedings concerning the Brasilia development in Guadalajara. The development comprises 328 residential units and 20 commercial units. It faced four simultaneous suspension orders promoted by neighbours, associations and PRODEUR, creating an immediate legal impediment to the continuation of construction and to the planned sale and delivery of the residential and commercial units.
The firm acted as defence counsel for IDEX - Brasilia in responding to the injunction proceedings and seeking the revocation of each of the four simultaneous suspension orders. The defence addressed the legal basis on which a project may be halted through suspension measures where environmental harm is alleged, while protecting the continued construction, sale and delivery of the 328-unit residential and 20-unit commercial development in Guadalajara. The arguments advanced required any alleged environmental harm to be substantiated, real, actual and imminent, rather than resting on unsupported allegations.
Within three weeks, the firm secured revocation of all four suspensions. The revocation of all four suspension orders enabled construction, sale and delivery of the Brasilia development to proceed. The proceedings established arguments requiring substantiated, real, actual and imminent environmental harm before a project may be halted, addressing the unsupported allegations advanced in support of the injunction proceedings and four simultaneous suspension orders promoted by neighbours, associations and PRODEUR.
