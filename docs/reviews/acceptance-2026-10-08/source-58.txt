Name of the Matter: | Confidential (Y/N): Y
Client: Nueva Empresa, S.C.
Matter’s Value (specify currency) and/or other key numbers: Approx. USD 171,494.21
Matter Status (closed in last year or ongoing?): ongoing
		
			Matter’s Context:
Nueva Empresa, S.C. engaged DeForest in two high-value wrongful termination proceedings that raised issues extending well beyond financial exposure. Following procedural objections concerning the legal standing of the company's attorney-in-fact, the labor court refused to recognise the company's representation, disregarded its defence and issued an adverse ruling despite the existence of valid notarial documentation confirming the attorney's legal identity.

The dispute presented a significant constitutional question concerning due process, legal certainty and the recognition of legally valid identity changes, creating an important opportunity to clarify procedural standards within Mexico's labor justice system.
		
		Firm’s role and main output:
DeForest challenged the ruling through constitutional amparo proceedings, arguing that the labor court's interpretation violated the client's due process rights and improperly disregarded legally recognised identity documentation. The Collegiate Court granted constitutional relief, ordered recognition of the attorney's legal standing and restored the client's right to present its defence.

Beyond the financial exposure of approximately MXN 3 million, the matter is significant for reinforcing constitutional protections governing procedural fairness and the legal recognition of identity, establishing an important precedent with broader implications for labor litigation in Mexico.
