Ramos Castillo protects the business value of real estate assets when regulatory intervention, environmental measures, expropriation or litigation threatens to halt a development, deprive an owner of its land or render an investment commercially unviable. Clients engage the team at the point of greatest exposure: when construction has been suspended, operating permits are under attack, title cannot be registered or a public authority has attempted to appropriate property without compensation.

Led by José Pablo Ramos Castillo, the practice has repeatedly converted complex constitutional, administrative and technical disputes into outcomes that preserve ownership, unlock projects and protect business continuity. In the El Cielo Country Club proceedings, José Pablo led the strategy protecting a development valued at MXN 3 billion (approximately USD 176.6 million) against successive environmental and land-use decrees. The team preserved previously granted development rights, secured appellate confirmation of the relief obtained and achieved enforcement of a further favourable judgment in July 2024. The result protected not only the underlying land and permits, but also the continued viability of the development and the position of its purchasers.

The same commercial focus defines the team’s work for Duranpark in Durango. Faced with the attempted expropriation of approximately 207.5 hectares forming part of the Durango Logistics and Industrial Center, Ramos Castillo secured a definitive suspension preventing measures affecting possession, title or registration. The intervention protected an asset valued at MXN 698.4 million (approximately USD 41.1 million) while preserving the client’s ability to pursue the project and defend its investment.

José Pablo’s strategic leadership is supported by Edgar Adrián Moro López and Mónica Dariane Cárdenas Fregoso. Edgar already assumes substantive responsibility for business-critical mandates, acting as lead associate in the Diageo México Operaciones dispute, where the team obtained precautionary relief allowing works and activities connected with an MXN 1 billion (approximately USD 58.9 million) agro-industrial facility to continue. Mónica provides continuity across the practice’s principal development, environmental, ownership and expropriation disputes, ensuring that the team retains command of the factual and technical record across related proceedings. This deliberately leveraged structure combines senior strategic judgment with genuine associate ownership and consistent execution.

The portfolio demonstrates results beyond Jalisco, including significant mandates in Durango and Guanajuato and challenges involving federal authorities and nationwide regulation. Ramos Castillo has protected developments, industrial facilities and privately owned land worth several billion Mexican pesos; reversed or neutralised measures that threatened construction and operations; and preserved clients’ ability to use, develop and monetise their assets while litigation continued. This is not merely a regional public-law practice handling real estate-related disputes. It is a national real estate disputes practice whose work protects the economics, continuity and long-term value of major projects across Mexico.

EL CIELO COUNTRY CLUB. A first class development located in the Bugambilias hill, where urbanity is combined with respect and coexistence with nature, making it one of the most attractive developments in the State of Jalisco.
D2 Summary of matter and your department's role – Please say why this matter was important. Also, tell us exactly what role your department played.
The Government of the State of Jalisco and the Municipality of Tlajomulco (in the same State), issued administrative Decrees, by means of which it was determined that El Cielo Country Club represents a Natural Protected Area. As a consequence of these Decrees, the urbanisation process of said area was stopped. The aforementioned implied a very serious impact on the property and a serious risk to the continuity and subsistence of the development, as well as to the property of the purchasers. Against such decrees, our team of lawyers filed two amparo lawsuits, one in 2018 and the other in 2021. In the first one, our firm achieved a very important resolution in the first instance, where it was recognized: i) the legality of the development; ii) the validity of the permits for a housing development granted prior to the Decrees that intended to disqualify the development; and above all, iii) that the development does not affect the environment, but on the contrary, it is environmentally friendly, sustainable and combines urbanisation with respect and coexistence with nature. This criterion is of great importance, not only for the company that maintains the development, its patrimony, its image and subsistence, but also for third parties. At the legal level, the work of our firm was key to set a very important precedent. In this way, it is established that it is not possible for political decisions to disregard land uses already granted and in execution, since the authority is obliged to respect the authorizations issued, which generates legal certainty for investment in urban development. This matter demonstrates that the environment is a sensitive issue and environmental measures must be technically based on scientific studies. The Amparo filed by our firm in 2021, is in the stage of integration of expert evidence, awaiting resolution in early 2024.

IDEX - Brasilia. Idex is an innovative company in the construction industry, leader in the State of Jalisco, with 45 years of experience in the area.
D2 Summary of matter and your department's role – Please say why this matter was important. Also, tell us exactly what role your department played.
IDEX, among its various projects, initiated the commercially denominated "Brasilia", located in the city of Guadalajara, Jalisco, consisting of a vertical development of 328 housing units and 20 commercial units. This development was the object of a serious attack by neighbors, neighborhood associations and the State Urban Development Attorney's Office (PRODEUR), who launched media, legal and jurisdictional attacks, alleging that "Brasilia" affected the correct urban development of the city, since it allegedly had a negative impact on traffic, concentration of inhabitants, water and electricity services and the enjoyment of the city, in addition to claiming alleged damage to groundwater. The aforementioned, without having any real legal basis, but simply the pretension of obtaining benefits through collective pressure. These organizations sought the closure, obstruction and non-execution of Brasilia by filing several injunction lawsuits, requesting suspensions and exerting pressure through the authorities. As a result, the development was the victim of four simultaneous suspensions that ordered the closure of the development, based on alleged environmental impacts and risks. These suspensions were the result of four different lawsuits that ordered the closure of the project to prevent its execution. Our team of lawyers managed to revoke the four suspensions in a period of 3 weeks, generating important legal criteria, such as: (i) the environmental impact must be sufficiently accredited as something real, actual and imminent and not only as a mere assumption without basis; (ii) although there is a Supreme Court criterion to immediately halt developments when an environmental impact is alleged, we achieved its application in a balanced manner, leaving established that whoever claims an environmental impact or risk must do so with sufficient grounds and not based on allegations without technical support. Our firm was able to establish a very important legal precedent, even though the Supreme Court of Justice of Mexico ruled against it. As a result, developers now have guarantees regarding the continuity of their operations. This case evidenced a clear opportunity to defend against generic or unfounded allegations of environmental damage.
